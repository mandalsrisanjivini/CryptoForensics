Bitcoin Blockchain Exclusively

CryptoForensics operates exclusively on authentic Bitcoin UTXO transaction data. All nodes represent Bitcoin transactions and all edges represent directed payment fund flows.

Pseudonymous Cryptographic Privacy

Bitcoin addresses and transactions are pseudonymous cryptographic hashes. In accordance with ethical forensic standards, no personal identities, physical names, bank accounts, or IP addresses are fabricated.

Unlabeled Does Not Mean Illicit

77.15% of transactions in the benchmark are unlabeled in the ground truth. These represent ordinary commercial and peer-to-peer Bitcoin transfers that serve as the target space for topological taint propagation and machine learning inference.

Ground-Truth vs Analytical Risk

The platform maintains strict ontological separation between ground-truth dataset classifications (confirmed historical law-enforcement seizures) and analytical risk assessments (probabilistic ML and network indicators).
